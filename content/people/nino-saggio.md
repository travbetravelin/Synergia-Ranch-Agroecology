Nino Saggio is an Italian architect and researcher exploring architectural and spatial responses to water in all its states — liquid, solid, and gaseous. His work examines how the built environment can adapt to, and work with, the crises and transformations of water in a changing climate.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], Nino presented on architectural thinking around [[water-management]], drawing connections between the design of space and the movement of water through landscapes and cities. His interdisciplinary perspective brings together architecture, ecology, and climate science to address [[desertification]] and water scarcity.

Nino's presentation is available online at his [personal research site](https://www.nitrosaggio.net/iquaderni/Conferenze/Biosphere/Water2026.html).
