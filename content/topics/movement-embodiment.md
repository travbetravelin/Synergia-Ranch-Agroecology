Movement and embodiment practices use somatic awareness, dance, and body-based learning to integrate ecological concepts through direct physical experience. Rather than approaching land and water stewardship as purely intellectual subjects, these practices invite participants to feel the patterns of growth, flow, and decay as lived experience.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], [[kristin-swiat]] led sessions exploring how earth-based metaphor and somatic movement can deepen ecological understanding. Her work draws on performance, bodywork, and [[regenerative-farming]] to create a holistic approach to land connection.

This thread of the conference sits alongside the more technical sessions on [[water-management]] and [[soil-health]], offering a complementary way of knowing rooted in presence and the body rather than abstraction.
