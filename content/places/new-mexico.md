New Mexico is a high-desert state in the American Southwest facing acute and accelerating challenges of aridification, water scarcity, and land degradation. It is also home to a remarkable concentration of regenerative practitioners, Indigenous land stewards, and ecological innovators working to reverse [[desertification]] and restore the land's capacity to hold water.

The state is home to [[synergia-ranch]] in Santa Fe, [[spirit-farm]] in Vanderwagen, and the Pueblo of Santa Ana's native plant nursery managed by [[mike-halverson]]. [[zack-withers]] works here on water sovereignty, and [[jan-willem-jansens]] practices ecological landscape planning based in Santa Fe.

The [[2026-water-conference]] was held at [[synergia-ranch]] in Santa Fe, drawing on New Mexico's unique position as both a frontline of climate-driven [[desertification]] and a hub of [[indigenous-knowledge]], [[water-management]] innovation, and [[regenerative-farming]] practice.
