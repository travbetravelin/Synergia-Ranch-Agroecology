Water management encompasses the full range of practices for stewarding water at landscape scale — from small-scale farm interventions to watershed-level planning and community water governance. In arid and semi-arid regions like [[new-mexico]], water management is foundational to everything else: without water, [[soil-health]], [[regenerative-farming]], and land restoration are impossible.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], water management was the central theme. [[jan-willem-jansens]] presented on landscape-scale watershed planning. [[indra-shekhar-singh]] explored decentralised, community-led [[river-revival]]. [[zack-withers]] addressed water sovereignty and the politics of water access. [[austin-unruh]] showed how [[agroforestry]] improves water infiltration, and [[amanda-bramble]] explored water's relationship with [[soil-health]] and [[erosion-control]].

Effective water management requires integrating [[indigenous-knowledge]], ecological planning, community governance, and on-the-ground practices like [[erosion-control]] and [[composting]] to restore the natural water cycle across degraded landscapes.
