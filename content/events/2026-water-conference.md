Water | Wind | Wisdom was a three-day international conference on regenerative solutions to [[desertification]], held July 17–19, 2026 at [[synergia-ranch]] in Santa Fe, [[new-mexico]]. It brought together farmers, ecologists, architects, Indigenous land stewards, community scientists, and movement practitioners from across the world.

The conference was convened around a central question: how do we restore the living water cycles of degraded landscapes? Presentations and workshops addressed [[water-management]], [[soil-health]], [[erosion-control]], [[biochar]], [[agroforestry]], [[indigenous-knowledge]], [[river-revival]], and [[movement-embodiment]].

**Speakers and presenters included:**

- [[mark-nelson]] — Institute of Ecotechnics, ecological engineering and ecosystem restoration
- [[indra-shekhar-singh]] — decentralised water management and [[river-revival]] in South Asia
- [[james-skeet]] and [[joyce-skeet]] — [[spirit-farm]], [[biochar]], [[botanical-knowledge]], and Navajo land stewardship
- [[austin-unruh]] — Trees for Graziers, [[agroforestry]] and silvopasture
- [[jan-willem-jansens]] — landscape-scale [[water-management]] and ecological planning
- [[amanda-bramble]] — Ampersand Sustainable Learning Center, land and water relationships
- [[mike-halverson]] — Santa Ana Native Plants, native plant restoration and [[indigenous-knowledge]]
- [[zack-withers]] — water sovereignty and community water rights in [[new-mexico]]
- [[kristin-swiat]] — [[movement-embodiment]] and somatic ecology
- [[nino-saggio]] — architectural responses to water and climate
- [[chili-hawes]] — director of [[synergia-ranch]], host and convener
