Austin Unruh is the founder of Trees for Graziers, an organisation that helps farmers and ranchers integrate trees into their grazing systems through [[agroforestry]] and silvopasture. His work bridges the gap between ecological restoration and working farm economics.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], Austin presented on how strategic tree placement supports [[water-management]], reduces [[erosion-control|erosion]], and rebuilds [[soil-health]] in grazed landscapes. He demonstrated how [[agroforestry]] systems can dramatically improve water infiltration and reduce runoff in semi-arid regions like [[new-mexico]].

Austin's approach to [[regenerative-farming]] treats trees not as obstacles but as infrastructure — for shade, fodder, water cycling, and long-term land resilience.
