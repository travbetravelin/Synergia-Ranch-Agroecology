Synergia Ranch is a 55-acre working ranch and ecological research site in Santa Fe, [[new-mexico]], and the home of the Institute of Ecotechnics. For over fifty years it has been a living laboratory for agroecological experimentation, community living, and ecological design. It is most widely known as the birthplace of the Biosphere 2 project.

The ranch hosted the [[2026-water-conference]], bringing together an international community of practitioners to explore regenerative solutions to [[desertification]]. [[chili-hawes]] serves as director and long-time steward, and [[mark-nelson]] of the Institute of Ecotechnics has been a central figure throughout its history.

Synergia Ranch practices include [[water-management]], [[composting]], [[soil-health]] restoration, and [[regenerative-farming]] across its high-desert landscape. [[kristin-swiat]] also works and practices at the ranch, connecting its land stewardship with somatic and [[movement-embodiment|embodiment]] work.
