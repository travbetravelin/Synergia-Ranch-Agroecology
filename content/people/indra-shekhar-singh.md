Indra Shekhar Singh is an Indian writer, advocate, and community scientist focused on decentralised [[water-management]] and [[river-revival]]. He champions low-tech, nature-based solutions rooted in [[indigenous-knowledge]] and community participation rather than large infrastructure projects.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], Indra presented on grassroots approaches to [[river-revival]] in South Asia, showing how communities using traditional water harvesting methods have restored rivers and reversed [[desertification]] without relying on centralised authority or expensive technology.

His work challenges the dominant paradigm of water governance, arguing that the answers to water scarcity already exist in place-based [[indigenous-knowledge]] systems — they simply need to be recognised and supported.
