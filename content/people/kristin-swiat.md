Kristin Swiat is a performer, bodyworker, and farm manager whose work sits at the intersection of somatic practice and ecological land stewardship. She explores how [[movement-embodiment|movement and embodiment]] can deepen our relationship with the living world.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], Kristin led sessions on [[movement-embodiment]], using earth-based metaphor and somatic practice to integrate ecological concepts through the body. Her work offers a counterpoint to purely analytical approaches, inviting participants to feel the patterns of water, soil, and growth as lived experience.

Her practice is rooted in [[synergia-ranch]] and connects the realms of performance, bodywork, and [[regenerative-farming]].
