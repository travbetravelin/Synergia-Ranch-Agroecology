Amanda Bramble is a presenter and educator at Ampersand Sustainable Learning Center, where she focuses on the relationship between land stewardship and water cycles. Her work emphasises hands-on learning as a path to ecological understanding.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], Amanda presented on practical approaches to [[water-management]] and [[soil-health]] in high-desert landscapes, exploring how [[erosion-control]] and regenerative land practices can reverse [[desertification]]. Her teaching integrates [[botanical-knowledge]] with direct experience of place.

Amanda is connected to the broader network of practitioners working at [[synergia-ranch]] and across [[new-mexico]].
