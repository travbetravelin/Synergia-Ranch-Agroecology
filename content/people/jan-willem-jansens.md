Jan-Willem Jansens is an ecological planner based in Santa Fe, [[new-mexico]], working with Ecotone Landscape Planning. His practice focuses on landscape-scale ecological restoration, watershed planning, and the design of living systems that work with natural water cycles.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], Jan-Willem presented on landscape-scale approaches to [[water-management]] and [[erosion-control]], with a focus on how thoughtful planning can reconnect degraded landscapes to healthy water cycles. His work integrates [[soil-health]], native vegetation, and watershed function into practical restoration plans.

His presence at [[synergia-ranch]] reflects a broader community of ecological designers and planners working to restore the high-desert landscapes of [[new-mexico]].
