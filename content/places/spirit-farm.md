Spirit Farm is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit farm in Vanderwagen, New Mexico, founded by [[james-skeet]] and [[joyce-skeet]]. Operating on Navajo land, the farm uses all-natural methods to restore degraded land, raise animals, grow food, and practice traditional land stewardship rooted in [[indigenous-knowledge]].

Spirit Farm is a working demonstration of [[regenerative-farming]] in the semi-arid Southwest, integrating [[biochar]], [[composting]], [[botanical-knowledge]], and native plant restoration to rebuild [[soil-health]] and reverse [[desertification]] on the land it stewards.

The farm is part of a growing network of Indigenous-led land restoration efforts across [[new-mexico]], and James and Joyce's participation in the [[2026-water-conference]] brought Spirit Farm's practice and perspective to a broader audience.
