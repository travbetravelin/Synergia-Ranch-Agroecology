Zack Withers is a water rights advocate and steward at Polk's Folly in [[new-mexico]], working to protect community access to water and resist the commodification of water resources. His work sits at the intersection of land stewardship, legal advocacy, and community organising.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], Zack presented on water sovereignty in [[new-mexico]], exploring how corporate and state water appropriation threatens small farmers, Indigenous communities, and ecosystems alike. His talk connected [[water-management]] to questions of justice, equity, and the long-term health of [[new-mexico]]'s watersheds.

His advocacy reflects a growing movement to treat water as a commons rather than a commodity — essential to any lasting approach to [[desertification]] and land regeneration.
