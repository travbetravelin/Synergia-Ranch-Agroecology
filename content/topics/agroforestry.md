Agroforestry is the intentional integration of trees into agricultural and grazing systems to create more resilient, productive, and ecologically rich landscapes. By combining trees with crops or livestock, agroforestry systems improve [[water-management]], [[soil-health]], biodiversity, and long-term land productivity.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], [[austin-unruh]] presented on silvopasture — a form of agroforestry that integrates trees into grazing land — demonstrating how tree canopy and root systems dramatically improve water infiltration and reduce runoff in semi-arid landscapes like [[new-mexico]].

Agroforestry is a cornerstone of [[regenerative-farming]] and plays an important role in reversing [[desertification]] and controlling [[erosion-control|erosion]] across degraded landscapes.
