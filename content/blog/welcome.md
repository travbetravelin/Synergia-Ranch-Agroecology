# Welcome to Synergia Ranch Agroecology

We are building something at Synergia Ranch that we believe matters deeply — a living demonstration of what regenerative land stewardship can look like in the high desert of New Mexico.

## What We Are Doing

Our work spans growing food, restoring land, and sharing knowledge. From building erosion control structures in the arroyos to propagating native plants in our new nursery, every practice is rooted in the same question: how do we work *with* the land rather than against it?

## Why It Matters

New Mexico is on the front lines of desertification. What we learn here — and share through workshops, conferences, and this site — has relevance far beyond the ranch.

We are glad you are here.
