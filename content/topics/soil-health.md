Soil health is the living foundation of all food systems and land stewardship. Healthy soil is teeming with microbial life, fungi, invertebrates, and organic matter — it absorbs water, sequesters carbon, and produces nutrient-dense food. Degraded soil is compacted, lifeless, and unable to hold water, contributing directly to [[desertification]] and [[erosion-control|erosion]].

Rebuilding soil health is central to [[regenerative-farming]] and involves practices like [[composting]], [[biochar]] application, cover cropping, and reduced tillage. [[agroforestry]] also plays a major role by cycling nutrients, protecting the soil surface, and feeding mycorrhizal networks.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], soil health was a through-line connecting presentations by [[austin-unruh]], [[james-skeet]], [[amanda-bramble]], [[mike-halverson]], and [[mark-nelson]]. [[synergia-ranch]] and [[spirit-farm]] are both long-running experiments in soil regeneration in the high-desert landscapes of [[new-mexico]].
