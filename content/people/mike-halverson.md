Mike Halverson is the nursery manager at Santa Ana Native Plants, operated by the Pueblo of Santa Ana in [[new-mexico]]. His work focuses on propagating native plants for ecological restoration, pollinator habitat, and cultural significance to Pueblo communities.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], Mike presented on the role of native plants in [[erosion-control]], [[water-management]], and [[soil-health]] restoration. He explored how locally sourced native species outperform introduced plants in restoration projects and carry deeper cultural meaning for the communities stewarding the land.

His work bridges [[botanical-knowledge]], [[indigenous-knowledge]], and practical horticulture, making native plants accessible for restoration projects across the region.
