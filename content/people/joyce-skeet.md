Joyce Skeet is co-founder of [[spirit-farm]] in Vanderwagen, New Mexico. Her work centres on native plants, herbal preparations, and the role of animals in regenerative land systems. She brings deep [[botanical-knowledge]] and a grounded, practical approach to land healing.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], Joyce presented on [[botanical-knowledge]] and herbal preparations rooted in Navajo tradition, exploring how native plants contribute to [[soil-health]], water retention, and ecological resilience. Her session also touched on the role of animals in [[regenerative-farming]] systems.

Joyce and [[james-skeet]] co-founded [[spirit-farm]] as a living demonstration of culturally grounded, all-natural agriculture in [[new-mexico]], drawing on [[indigenous-knowledge]] to guide every aspect of land stewardship.
