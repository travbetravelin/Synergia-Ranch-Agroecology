Botanical knowledge encompasses the understanding of native and cultivated plants — their ecology, medicinal and nutritional uses, cultural significance, and role in land restoration and food systems. It sits at the intersection of science, tradition, and place-based observation.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], both [[joyce-skeet]] and [[mike-halverson]] drew on deep botanical knowledge in their presentations. Joyce explored herbal preparations rooted in Navajo tradition, while Mike presented on native plant propagation for ecological restoration and pollinator habitat.

Botanical knowledge is closely linked to [[indigenous-knowledge]] and plays a practical role in [[erosion-control]], [[soil-health]], and the restoration of degraded landscapes across [[new-mexico]].
