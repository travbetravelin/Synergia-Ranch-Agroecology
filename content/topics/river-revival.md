River revival refers to community-led efforts to restore degraded rivers and watersheds using low-tech, nature-based methods and place-based knowledge. It prioritises community participation over large infrastructure, and [[indigenous-knowledge]] over purely technical interventions.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], [[indra-shekhar-singh]] presented on river revival movements in South Asia, where communities using traditional water harvesting systems have brought seasonal rivers back to life and reversed [[desertification]] across large areas. His work shows that local knowledge and collective action can achieve what expensive infrastructure often cannot.

River revival connects to [[water-management]], [[erosion-control]], and [[soil-health]] — healthy rivers require and support healthy land, and the relationship runs in both directions.
