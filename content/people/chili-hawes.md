Chili Hawes is the director and long-time steward of [[synergia-ranch]], where she has overseen decades of agroecological experimentation and community building. Her background spans systems thinking, organisational management, and ecological land stewardship.

Chili hosted the [[2026-water-conference]] at [[synergia-ranch]] and has been central to building the ranch as a hub for regenerative practice in [[new-mexico]]. She works at the intersection of community, land, and long-term ecological vision.

Her stewardship of Synergia connects threads of [[water-management]], [[soil-health]], [[composting]], and [[regenerative-farming]] that run through the ranch's decades of practice.
