James Skeet is co-founder of [[spirit-farm]] in Vanderwagen, New Mexico, and Covenant Pathways. A Navajo practitioner and land steward, he works at the intersection of [[indigenous-knowledge]], [[biochar]], and [[regenerative-farming]].

At the [[2026-water-conference]], James presented on the use of [[biochar]] as a tool for land and water regeneration in the semi-arid Southwest, drawing on both traditional Navajo land wisdom and contemporary research. His talk explored how [[biochar]] improves [[soil-health]] and water retention in degraded soils, and how [[indigenous-knowledge]] systems offer durable frameworks for addressing [[desertification]].

James and [[joyce-skeet]] have built [[spirit-farm]] into a working model of all-natural, culturally grounded land restoration in [[new-mexico]].
