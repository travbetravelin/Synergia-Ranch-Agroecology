Mark Nelson is the director of the Institute of Ecotechnics, a long-time steward of [[synergia-ranch]], and one of the original crew of Biosphere 2. He is co-designer of the Eden in Iraq wastewater restoration project and has spent decades working at the frontier of closed ecological systems and ecosystem restoration.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], Mark presented on large-scale ecosystem restoration and the lessons learned from decades of ecological engineering — including the role of [[water-management]] in reversing [[desertification]] and restoring degraded landscapes. He explored how the principles behind Biosphere 2 apply to real-world land restoration.

Mark's work connects [[synergia-ranch]] to a global network of ecological thinkers and practitioners. His deep knowledge of [[soil-health]] and closed-system ecology informs his ongoing work in [[new-mexico]] and beyond.
