title: Regenerative Farming
type: topic

---

Regenerative farming describes agricultural practices that restore rather than deplete — rebuilding [[soil-health]], biodiversity, and water cycles over time. It moves beyond sustainability (doing less harm) toward active regeneration of the living systems that underpin food production.

Core practices include [[composting]], [[biochar]] application, [[agroforestry]], cover cropping, rotational grazing, and [[erosion-control]]. Together these practices rebuild soil biology, improve [[water-management]], and reverse [[desertification]] in degraded landscapes.

At the [[2026-water-conference]], regenerative farming was a central theme, explored by [[austin-unruh]], [[james-skeet]], [[joyce-skeet]], [[amanda-bramble]], and others. [[synergia-ranch]] and [[spirit-farm]] both serve as working demonstrations of regenerative farming in the high-desert landscapes of [[new-mexico]].
