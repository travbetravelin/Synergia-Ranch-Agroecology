Composting is the process of building biological fertility through managed decomposition of organic matter. Healthy compost restores [[soil-health]], feeds soil microbial communities, improves water retention, and reduces the need for external inputs.

Methods range from simple hot composts and worm bins to more advanced systems like Johnson-Su bioreactors and [[biochar]] integration. When combined with [[biochar]], compost creates a long-lasting, biologically active soil amendment that supports [[regenerative-farming]] and [[water-management]] at the landscape scale.

[[synergia-ranch]] has practiced composting as part of its agroecological system for decades, and it features as a core practice in addressing [[desertification]] and rebuilding depleted soils across [[new-mexico]].
